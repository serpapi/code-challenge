Current developments regarding the conflict with Iran include:
  • Interim Agreement: A memorandum of understanding (MoU) signed last week calls for an immediate cessation of military operations on all fronts, including Lebanon. Mediators from Pakistan and Qatar report "encouraging progress" toward a final peace deal.

  • Sanctions and Assets: The U.S. Treasury has issued a 60-day waiver allowing Iranian oil exports until August 21. Progress has also been reported on the release of $12 billion in frozen Iranian assets.
