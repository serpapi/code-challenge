Description

Cricket is a bat-and-ball game that is played between two teams of eleven players on a field, at the centre of which is a 22-yard pitch with a wicket at each end, each comprising two bails balanced on three stumps. Wikipedia
Country or region: Worldwide (most popular in the Commonwealth)
Equipment: Cricket ball, Cricket bat, Wicket (Stumps, Bails), Protective equipment
