Description

Norman Percevel Rockwell was an American painter and illustrator. His works have a broad popular appeal in the United States for their reflection of the country's culture. Rockwell is most famous for the cover illustrations of everyday life he created for The Saturday Evening Post magazine over nearly five decades. Wikipedia
Born: February 3, 1894, New York, NY
Died: November 8, 1978 (age 84 years), Stockbridge, MA
Spouse: Molly Punderson (m. 1961–1978), Mary Barstow (m. 1930–1959), Irene O'Connor (m. 1916–1930)
Education: The Art Students League of New York · See more
Children: Thomas Rockwell, Peter Barstow
Parents: Jarvis Waring Rockwell, Anne Mary Rockwell
