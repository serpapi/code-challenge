About

Description

Andy Warhol was an American visual artist, film director and producer. A leading figure in the pop art movement, Warhol is considered the most important artist of the second half of the 20th century. Wikipedia
Born: 6 August 1928, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, United States
Died: 22 February 1987 (age 58 years), New York Presbyterian Hospital, New York, United States
Periods: Pop art, Modern art
Influenced by: Marcel Duchamp, Jasper Johns, Truman Capote, Tom of Finland, Ben Shahn, Jack Smith
Education: Schenley Apartments (1942–1945), Carnegie Mellon School of Art
