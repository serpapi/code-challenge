About

Description

Rembrandt Harmenszoon van Rijn, usually simply known as Rembrandt, was a Dutch Golden Age painter, printmaker, and draughtsman. He is generally considered one of the greatest visual artists in the history of art. Wikipedia
Born: 15 July 1606, Leiden, Netherlands
Died: 4 October 1669 (age 63 years), Amsterdam, Netherlands
Periods: Baroque, Baroque painting, Dutch Golden Age
Spouse: Saskia van Uylenburgh (m. 1634–1642)
Influenced by: Caravaggio, Titian, Hercules Seghers, Hendrick ter Brugghen
Full name: Rembrandt Harmenszoon van Rijn
