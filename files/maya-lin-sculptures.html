    Description

    Maya Ying Lin is an American architect, designer, and sculptor. Born in Athens, Ohio to Chinese immigrants, she attended Yale University to study architecture. She has an older brother, the poet Tan Lin. Wikipedia
    Born: October 5, 1959 (age 66 years), Athens, OH
    Spouse: Daniel Wolf
    Children: 2
    Parents: Julia Chang Lin, Henry Huan Lin
    Education: Yale School of Architecture (1986), Yale University (1981), Athens High School, Saybrook College
    Known for: Land art, architecture, memorials
    Awards: National Medal of Arts Presidential Medal of Freedom
