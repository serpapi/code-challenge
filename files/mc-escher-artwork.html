    About

    Description

    Maurits Cornelis Escher was a Dutch graphic artist who made woodcuts, lithographs, and mezzotints, many of which were inspired by mathematics. Despite wide popular interest, for most of his life Escher was neglected in the art world, even in his native Netherlands. He was 70 before a retrospective exhibition was held. Wikipedia
    Born: June 17, 1898, Leeuwarden, Netherlands
    Died: March 27, 1972 (age 73 years), Hilversum, Netherlands
    Period: Modern art
    Children: Giorgio Arnaldo Escher, Jan Escher, Arthur Escher
    Parents: George Arnold Escher, Sara Adriana Gleichman
    Education: School for Architecture and Decorative Arts (1919–1922), Delft University of Technology (TU Delft)
    Siblings: Berend George Escher
